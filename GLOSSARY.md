# CYOA

A fantasy adventure in which the player explores paths and encounters a dragon or merchant.

## Language

**Merchant**:
A character encountered when traveling south who offers a free choice of magical armor or a magical weapon. The player may leave without accepting an upgrade.
_Avoid_: Shopkeeper, vendor

**Magical armor**:
An upgrade that reduces damage received from successful enemy hits.
_Avoid_: Dodge bonus, extra health

**Magical weapon**:
A weapon enhanced to deal increased damage.
_Avoid_: Strength bonus
